### Documents to prep

- Passport copy + residency card
- Proof of funds letter translated into Danish
- Bank reference covering at least 24 months of relationship history
- Signed intent explaining investment purpose (rental vs. vacation use)

### Timeline at a glance

| Week | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Engage local solicitor + tax advisor |
| 2 | Submit KYC package to preferred bank |
| 3 | Lock preliminary mortgage term sheet |
| 4 | Schedule valuation + draft SPA |

> The offer that lands with financing proof, compliance docs, and a draft closing schedule beats higher bids that still need to "check with the bank."

### Bonus tip

Create a secure Notion or Dropbox space holding PDFs, bank contacts, and notarized translations. Share view-only access with the listing agent to demonstrate readiness without emailing sensitive attachments repeatedly.
