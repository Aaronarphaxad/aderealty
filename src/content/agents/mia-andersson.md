## About Mia

Mia Andersson's commitment to sustainable living stems from her background in environmental science and her personal dedication to reducing humanity's ecological impact. Over her 10-year career, she has become a leading voice in green real estate in Scandinavia.

Mia's expertise covers everything from passive house design to renewable energy systems, ensuring that her clients not only find beautiful homes but also properties that contribute positively to the environment. She works closely with builders, architects, and environmental consultants to verify green certifications and performance claims.

When she's not helping clients find their dream eco-home, Mia enjoys hiking in Sweden's national parks and maintaining her own off-grid cabin. She also writes a popular blog about sustainable living, sharing tips and insights with a growing community of environmentally conscious readers.
