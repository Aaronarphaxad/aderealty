## The brief

Iker and his family were moving back to Bilbao and needed to sell their 1890s townhouse in central Odense before the school year started.

## What we did

- A valuation within 48 hours, based on six recent sales on the same street
- New photos and a floor plan in the first week, with the original beams and stair in every shot
- Two open houses on consecutive Sundays

## The result

Three offers after the second open house. The townhouse sold for €561,000 against an asking price of €545,000, nineteen days after it went live.
