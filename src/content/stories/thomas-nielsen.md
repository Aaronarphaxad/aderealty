## The brief

Thomas and his wife wanted to swap their family house outside Aarhus for an apartment near the water, without paying for two homes at once.

## What we did

- Listed the house and searched for the apartment at the same time
- Shortlisted four apartments in Aarhus Ø with lifts and south-facing balconies
- Agreed a completion date that matched the sale of the house

## The result

They bought a three-bedroom harbour apartment for €872,000, below the €890,000 asking price, and moved in the week the house was handed over.
