This exceptional lakeside villa offers the quintessential Finnish dream: a modern home with direct access to pristine lake waters. Built with sustainability and comfort in mind, every detail has been carefully considered.

Located in the prestigious Pyynikki area, you're surrounded by nature yet close to Tampere's vibrant city center. The famous Pyynikki observation tower and its legendary doughnuts are just a short walk away.
