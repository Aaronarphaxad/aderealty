Whether you're buying or selling property, understanding the complete process helps you make informed decisions and avoid surprises. This guide walks you through every step of both journeys.

---

## The home buying process

### Phase 1: Preparation (2-4 Weeks)

#### Step 1: Assess Your Financial Situation

- Review your credit score and report
- Calculate your budget and savings
- Determine down payment amount
- Estimate monthly housing costs
- Factor in additional expenses

#### Step 2: Get Pre-Approved for a Mortgage

- Research and compare lenders
- Gather financial documents
- Submit pre-approval application
- Receive pre-approval letter
- Understand your borrowing capacity

#### Step 3: Define Your Criteria

- List must-have features
- Identify preferred locations
- Determine property type
- Consider future needs
- Set realistic expectations

#### Step 4: Assemble Your Team

- Select a real estate agent
- Choose a lender
- Identify home inspector
- Consider attorney (if needed)
- Find insurance agent

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks  
**Cost:** Usually free (except possible credit report fee)

---

### Phase 2: Property Search (2-8 Weeks)

#### Step 5: Begin Property Search

- Review online listings
- Attend open houses
- Schedule private showings
- Explore neighborhoods
- Take notes and photos

#### Step 6: Evaluate Properties

- Compare features and prices
- Consider location factors
- Assess condition
- Evaluate long-term value
- Review homeowner association rules

#### Step 7: Narrow Your Options

- Create shortlist of favorites
- Schedule second viewings
- Bring family/friends for input
- Ask detailed questions
- Visualize living there

**Timeline:** Varies widely, typically 2-8 weeks  
**Cost:** Free

---

### Phase 3: Making an Offer (1-2 Weeks)

#### Step 8: Conduct Additional Research

- Review comparable sales
- Research neighborhood trends
- Understand seller's situation
- Check property history
- Review disclosure documents

#### Step 9: Determine Offer Strategy

- Decide on offer price
- Plan contingencies
- Consider earnest money amount
- Determine closing timeline
- Decide on included items

#### Step 10: Submit Your Offer

- Complete purchase agreement
- Include pre-approval letter
- Submit earnest money deposit
- Specify contingencies
- Set response deadline

#### Step 11: Negotiate

- Review seller's response
- Prepare counteroffers if needed
- Stay within your budget
- Remain objective
- Reach mutual agreement

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks  
**Cost:** Earnest money deposit (1-3% of purchase price, applied to closing)

---

### Phase 4: Under Contract (30-45 Days)

#### Step 12: Open Escrow

- Sign purchase agreement
- Deposit earnest money
- Title search begins
- Schedule inspections
- Begin mortgage application

#### Step 13: Complete Home Inspection

- Hire professional inspector
- Attend inspection if possible
- Review detailed report
- Identify major concerns
- Decide on repair requests

#### Step 14: Request Repairs or Credits

- Prioritize safety and structural issues
- Submit repair request
- Negotiate with seller
- Reach agreement
- Confirm repairs in writing

#### Step 15: Complete Mortgage Application

- Submit full application
- Provide all documentation
- Schedule property appraisal
- Respond to lender requests
- Monitor application progress

#### Step 16: Property Appraisal

- Lender orders appraisal
- Appraiser evaluates property
- Review appraisal report
- Address low appraisal if occurs
- Lender reviews for approval

#### Step 17: Finalize Financing

- Complete underwriting
- Meet any conditions
- Receive clear to close
- Lock interest rate
- Review closing disclosure

#### Step 18: Final Walk-Through

- Inspect property 1-2 days before closing
- Verify repairs completed
- Check included items present
- Ensure property condition unchanged
- Document any issues immediately

**Timeline:** 30-45 days  
**Cost:** Home inspection ($300-500), appraisal ($400-600)

---

### Phase 5: Closing (1-2 Days)

#### Step 19: Review Closing Documents

- Receive closing disclosure (3 days before)
- Review all costs and fees
- Verify loan terms
- Check property details
- Note any discrepancies

#### Step 20: Prepare for Closing

- Arrange certified funds
- Bring valid ID
- Review final figures
- Confirm closing location
- Schedule time off work

#### Step 21: Attend Closing

- Sign all documents
- Transfer funds
- Receive keys
- Get copies of all documents
- Celebrate your new home!

**Timeline:** 1-2 days  
**Cost:** Down payment + closing costs (2-5% of purchase price)

---

### Phase 6: Post-Purchase (First 30 Days)

#### Step 22: Move In

- Change locks
- Set up utilities
- Update address
- Test systems
- Meet neighbors

#### Step 23: Handle Administrative Tasks

- File homestead exemption
- Set up homeowners insurance
- Register with HOA (if applicable)
- Organize important documents
- Create maintenance schedule

**Timeline:** First 30 days  
**Cost:** Moving expenses, immediate purchases

---

## The home selling process

### Phase 1: Preparation (2-6 Weeks)

#### Step 1: Decide to Sell

- Evaluate market conditions
- Assess your readiness
- Understand your equity
- Plan your next move
- Set timeline goals

#### Step 2: Choose a Real Estate Agent

- Interview multiple agents
- Review track records
- Discuss marketing strategies
- Understand commission structure
- Sign listing agreement

#### Step 3: Determine Asking Price

- Review comparative market analysis
- Consider recent sales
- Evaluate your property's condition
- Understand market trends
- Set strategic pricing

#### Step 4: Prepare Your Home

**Repairs:**

- Fix obvious problems
- Address safety issues
- Improve curb appeal
- Update dated features
- Complete necessary maintenance

**Cleaning:**

- Deep clean entire home
- Clean carpets and windows
- Organize and declutter
- Remove personal items
- Create neutral environment

**Staging:**

- Arrange furniture optimally
- Enhance lighting
- Add neutral decor
- Create inviting spaces
- Maximize appeal

#### Step 5: Professional Photography

- Hire experienced photographer
- Stage for photos
- Capture best features
- Include exterior shots
- Create virtual tour

**Timeline:** 2-6 weeks  
**Cost:** Repairs, cleaning, staging (varies), photography ($300-500)

---

### Phase 2: Marketing (Ongoing)

#### Step 6: List Your Property

- Enter into MLS
- Post on major websites
- Create marketing materials
- Install yard sign
- Launch social media campaign

#### Step 7: Host Open Houses

- Schedule strategically
- Prepare home for visitors
- Provide information sheets
- Create welcoming atmosphere
- Collect feedback

#### Step 8: Manage Showings

- Stay flexible with timing
- Keep home showing-ready
- Leave during showings
- Provide easy access
- Maintain cleanliness

#### Step 9: Track and Adjust

- Monitor showing activity
- Gather buyer feedback
- Assess market response
- Adjust strategy if needed
- Consider price adjustments

**Timeline:** Until offer accepted  
**Cost:** Utilities, maintenance during listing period

---

### Phase 3: Receiving and Evaluating Offers (1-2 Weeks)

#### Step 10: Review Offers

- Evaluate all components
- Consider buyer qualifications
- Assess contingencies
- Review proposed timeline
- Check earnest money amount

#### Step 11: Respond to Offers

- Accept, counter, or reject
- Negotiate terms
- Consider multiple offers
- Communicate through agent
- Reach agreement

#### Step 12: Accept an Offer

- Sign purchase agreement
- Receive earnest money
- Begin escrow process
- Prepare for inspections
- Coordinate with your agent

**Timeline:** Varies, could be days to weeks  
**Cost:** None

---

### Phase 4: Under Contract (30-45 Days)

#### Step 13: Facilitate Home Inspection

- Schedule convenient time
- Make property accessible
- Await inspection report
- Review buyer's requests
- Negotiate repairs or credits

#### Step 14: Complete Agreed Repairs

- Hire qualified contractors
- Obtain necessary permits
- Complete work on time
- Document repairs
- Provide receipts and warranties

#### Step 15: Coordinate Appraisal

- Allow appraiser access
- Provide property information
- Highlight improvements
- Supply comparable sales
- Address low appraisal if occurs

#### Step 16: Respond to Buyer Requests

- Provide requested documents
- Answer questions promptly
- Facilitate additional showings
- Cooperate with lender
- Maintain communication

#### Step 17: Plan Your Move

- Schedule movers
- Begin packing
- Arrange utilities transfer
- Update addresses
- Coordinate timing with purchase

**Timeline:** 30-45 days  
**Cost:** Negotiated repairs, moving expenses

---

### Phase 5: Closing (1-2 Days)

#### Step 18: Complete Final Preparations

- Remove all personal property
- Complete final cleaning
- Leave agreed items
- Gather all keys and remotes
- Prepare for final walk-through

#### Step 19: Attend Final Walk-Through

- Buyer inspects property
- Verify repairs completed
- Confirm property condition
- Address last-minute concerns
- Resolve any issues

#### Step 20: Review Closing Documents

- Examine settlement statement
- Verify payoff amounts
- Understand all credits and debits
- Check tax prorations
- Note any discrepancies

#### Step 21: Close the Sale

- Sign all documents
- Transfer ownership
- Receive proceeds
- Hand over keys
- Cancel homeowners insurance

**Timeline:** 1-2 days  
**Cost:** Real estate commission (typically 5-6%), closing costs (1-3%)

---

### Phase 6: Post-Sale (First 30 Days)

#### Step 22: Complete Administrative Tasks

- Cancel utilities
- Forward mail
- File appropriate taxes
- Keep closing documents
- Update address with institutions

#### Step 23: Financial Considerations

- Deposit proceeds
- Pay off outstanding liens
- Consult tax advisor
- Plan for capital gains taxes
- Update financial plans

**Timeline:** First 30 days  
**Cost:** Tax implications (if applicable)

---

## Simultaneous buying and selling

Many people need to sell one property while buying another. Here's how to coordinate:

### Strategy 1: Sell First, Then Buy

**Advantages:**

- Know your budget
- Stronger buying position
- Less stress
- No double mortgage

**Disadvantages:**

- Temporary housing may be needed
- Could miss perfect property
- Market may change

### Strategy 2: Buy First, Then Sell

**Advantages:**

- No temporary housing
- Time to prepare sale property
- Less rushed

**Disadvantages:**

- Need significant funds
- Risk of two mortgages
- More stressful
- Contingent on sale

### Strategy 3: Contingent Offers

**How It Works:**

- Purchase contingent on selling current home
- Protects you financially
- Common in balanced markets

**Considerations:**

- Less attractive to sellers
- May lose in multiple offers
- Could delay both transactions

### Strategy 4: Bridge Financing

**How It Works:**

- Short-term loan bridges gap
- Uses equity from current home
- Pay off when home sells

**Considerations:**

- Higher interest rates
- Additional costs
- Requires significant equity
- Time-limited

## Important timelines and deadlines

**Never Miss These Deadlines:**

- Inspection contingency removal
- Financing contingency removal
- Appraisal contingency removal
- Title review period
- Final walk-through
- Closing date

**Consequences of Missing Deadlines:**

- Lose earnest money
- Breach of contract
- Legal liability
- Transaction cancellation
