## The playbook

1. **Pre-tour intel**: send a two-question Typeform about morning routines and work needs. Build the tour around their answers.
2. **Route planning**: mix listings with lifestyle stops (third-wave coffee, bike lanes, co-working entry) to show how a day flows.
3. **Field kit**: tote with bottled water, printed floor plans, and a QR card linking to digital disclosures.

### Script the transitions

Use markdown blocks to stay on message:

```markdown
- Anchor each stop with a takeaway: "This duplex keeps the nursery close yet separate."
- Ask buyers to rate the home out of 10 immediately to capture gut reactions.
- Close every car ride with "What surprised you?" to surface objections early.
```

### Aftercare email template

> **Subject:** Your curated day in Frederiksberg
>
> Loved showing you around today. Here are the private links to the three homes plus a map of the cafés we visited. I've also penciled in two twilight showings for Friday if you'd like a different vibe.

When buyers feel seen beyond the square meters, they stop comparison shopping agents and start planning offers with you.
