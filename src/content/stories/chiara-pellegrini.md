## The brief

Chiara was relocating from Milan to Stockholm and wanted a waterfront apartment in the Old Town, with only one trip north to decide.

## What we did

- Toured five apartments on video calls before her visit
- Booked the two best for a single afternoon
- Coordinated the contract and the bank in English and Swedish

## The result

Chiara bought the three-bedroom waterfront apartment for €1,280,000, nine days after it was listed, and moved in six weeks later.
