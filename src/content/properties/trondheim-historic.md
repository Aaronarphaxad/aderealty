Step back in time with this beautifully restored townhouse in Bakklandet, Trondheim's most charming historic neighborhood. Dating from 1850, this property has been lovingly renovated to preserve its period character while incorporating modern conveniences.

The home's original wooden beams, vintage details, and traditional layout tell stories of centuries past, while thoughtful updates ensure comfortable contemporary living. Each room has its own unique character, from cozy window seats to elegant proportions.

Living on this cobblestone street means being part of Trondheim's living history. Colorful wooden houses, artisan cafés, and the Old Town Bridge create a picture-perfect setting, while the city center and cathedral are just across the river.
