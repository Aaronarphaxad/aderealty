## Retrofit priorities

1. **Heat pumps + smart thermostats**: show buyers cost savings with a simple before/after utility chart.
2. **Triple-glazed windows**: highlight decibel reduction for city dwellings.
3. **Recycled-material kitchens**: pair bamboo cabinetry with quartz alternatives for a premium feel.

### Marketing angle

```markdown
- Lead with data: "Energy rating jumped from D to B, estimated savings 9,800 DKK/year."
- Tell a micro-story: "The owners sourced Italian terrazzo made from factory offcuts."
- Offer continuity: include vendor contacts so buyers can replicate the palette elsewhere in the home.
```

### On-site storytelling

Set up placards during showings detailing each upgrade, QR-coded to deeper documentation. Buyers linger longer when they can interact with tangible proof of value.
