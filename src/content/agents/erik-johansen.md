## About Erik

Erik Johansen's fascination with historic architecture began during his childhood explorations of Norway's coastal heritage sites. This passion led him to study architectural history before entering the real estate field 14 years ago.

Erik's expertise extends beyond sales to include deep knowledge of building codes, preservation techniques, and period-appropriate renovations. He works closely with architects, historians, and craftsmen to ensure that historic properties are maintained and enhanced responsibly.

Beyond his professional work, Erik is an active member of several heritage preservation organizations. He volunteers his time teaching workshops on home maintenance for historic properties and enjoys woodworking, often creating custom pieces for the homes he sells.
