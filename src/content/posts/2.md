### Start with a radius, not a city

Pricing within central Copenhagen shifts every 400 meters. Instead of quotes like "Østerbro average is...", narrow the dataset:

```text
SELECT * FROM comps
WHERE postal_code = 2100
AND distance_from_subject <= 0.5km
AND sale_date >= CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '180 days';
```

### Metrics we watch weekly

| Metric | Sweet Spot | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Median DOM | 22 days | Signals if buyer urgency is cooling |
| Sale-to-list | 101% | Guides whether to list at, under, or over ask |
| Price per sqm | 64,000 DKK | Normalizes renovation differences |

> Momentum at the micro level lets you explain pricing logic to sellers with confidence, and negotiate price adjustments with receipts.

### Tell the story in your listing presentation

1. **Map the comps** to show the tight radius visually.
2. **Highlight outliers** (new builds vs. heritage flats) so the client understands value adjustments.
3. **Translate numbers into moves**: e.g., "We'll release at 6.45M and trigger private showings on day 5." 

Pairing clear visuals with narrative makes the client feel part of the strategy, not just the subject of it.
