## The brief

Sofia was buying her first home and wanted a bright two-bedroom apartment by the water in Malmö, with low running costs.

## What we did

- Arranged a mortgage pre-approval before the first viewing
- Explained the energy class and the monthly fee of every apartment on her list
- Advised on a single, firm bid instead of a long bidding round

## The result

Sofia bought the two-bedroom Västra Hamnen apartment for €612,000, fourteen days after it was listed.
