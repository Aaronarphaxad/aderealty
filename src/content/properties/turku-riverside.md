The interior is flooded with natural light, thanks to large windows that also provide lovely views of the River Aura. The efficient layout maximizes space while maintaining a sense of openness and flow between rooms.

Located along Finland's oldest river, you'll enjoy morning walks along the riverside path, with the city's medieval castle, cathedral, and vibrant market square all within easy reach.
