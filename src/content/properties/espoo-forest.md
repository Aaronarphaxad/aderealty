Experience the vision of Tapiola, Finland's famous garden city, in this charming mid-century home. Set on a generous forest plot, this property exemplifies the harmonious integration of architecture and nature that defines this iconic neighborhood.

The home retains its original 1960s charm, from the fireplace to the flowing layout, while recent updates have modernized the essential systems. Large windows bring the forest inside, creating a peaceful retreat that feels worlds away from urban life.

Despite the natural setting, you're connected to everything. The metro whisks you to Helsinki in 15 minutes, while local schools, shops, and the beautiful Tapiola central park are all within walking distance. This is suburban living done right.
