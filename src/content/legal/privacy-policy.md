## Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Adebiyi Aderinto, REALTOR® with Katrina & The Team, brokered by eXp Realty of Canada Inc. ("we", "us" or "Ade"), collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website or contact us.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

## Information we collect

We may collect the following personal information:

### Identity and contact details

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Preferred area, budget and what you are interested in (buying, selling or investing)

### Website and technical data

- Internet protocol (IP) address
- Browser type and device information
- Pages visited and how you reached the site

### Transaction and inquiry details

- Notes from discovery calls, emails or form submissions
- Property preferences and questions you send us

We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

## How we use your information

We use personal information only when we have a lawful reason to do so, including to:

- Respond to your inquiries and book discovery calls
- Provide real estate advice and services you have requested
- Send follow-up information related to your request
- Improve the website and understand how visitors use it
- Meet legal, regulatory and brokerage requirements

We do not sell your personal information.

## Sharing your information

We may share personal information with:

- Katrina & The Team and eXp Realty of Canada Inc., as needed to serve you
- Service providers who help run this website (for example hosting or form delivery)
- Professionals you ask us to introduce you to, such as mortgage brokers or lawyers
- Authorities when the law requires it

Third parties that receive your information are expected to protect it and use it only for the purpose we share it.

## Cookies and analytics

This site may use essential cookies that keep the site working. If Google Analytics or similar tools are added later, they may collect anonymous usage data. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law and brokerage record-keeping rules. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

## Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to:

- See the personal information we hold about you
- Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
- Withdraw consent for marketing communications
- Ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.
