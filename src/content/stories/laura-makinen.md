## The brief

Laura wanted a family house near the forest in Espoo, within a short walk of a school, after losing two homes in bidding rounds on her own.

## What we did

- Set alerts for Tapiola and two neighbouring districts, including homes before they went public
- Compared the house with the last eight sales in the garden city
- Handled the offer and the survey in a single week

## The result

Laura bought the four-bedroom Tapiola house for €860,000 against an asking price of €875,000, twenty-six days after it was listed.
